Decided 2026-11-11: monetize city data through citizens and small professionals, never government ("Gov is a bad
customer"). Small cost, high frequency, subscribe-able.

First step built: the **Pune DP report** (/pune/dp-report/ + one indexed page per locality): satellite imagery with
the traced DP zones / registered DP sheets, click a plot for its zone, reservations and DP roads. The viewer is free
and indexed; the PDF download asks for an email sign-in (Supabase OTP) and is free; downloads are logged in
`dp_reports` as the lead list.

**Why:** the free indexed pages are the SEO funnel and the email gate builds the list; paid tiers come later
(report packs for brokers, architects and small builders; society redevelopment reports; area alerts).
**How to apply:** keep the viewer and the pages free and crawlable; gate only the downloads and extras. More DP
coverage (merged villages, PCMC, Mumbai DP 2034) means more inventory. Related: [[editor-vision]]
